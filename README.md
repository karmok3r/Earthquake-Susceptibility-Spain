# Earthquake Susceptibility Analysis - Spain

A comprehensive GIS-based analysis of earthquake susceptibility across Spain, integrating seismic hazard factors, geological features, and spatial analysis techniques to identify high-risk zones.

![Earthquake Susceptibility Map](https://github.com/karmok3r/Earthquake-Susceptibility-Spain/blob/main/Earthquake%20Susceptibility%20Analysis.jpg)

## 🗺️ Project Overview

This project evaluates earthquake susceptibility across Spain by analyzing multiple geospatial factors including seismic activity, fault lines, geological formations, and proximity to tectonic boundaries. The analysis provides valuable insights for disaster preparedness and infrastructure planning.

## 📊 Methodology

### 1. Data Collection
- Seismic event data from geological surveys
- Fault line and tectonic boundary datasets
- Geological formation maps
- Population density and infrastructure data

### 2. Spatial Analysis
- **Weighted Overlay Analysis** - Combined multiple risk factors with appropriate weights
- **Proximity Analysis** - Distance calculations to fault lines and seismic epicenters
- **Kernel Density Estimation** - Identified seismic activity hotspots
- **Raster Analysis** - Processed elevation, slope, and geological formations

### 3. Risk Classification
Created susceptibility zones based on composite scores:
- **Very High**: Critical risk areas requiring immediate attention
- **High**: Significant risk zones with elevated seismic activity
- **Moderate**: Areas with medium susceptibility
- **Low**: Minimal risk zones
- **Very Low**: Negligible earthquake risk

## 📈 Results & Insights

The analysis reveals that:

1. **Southern and southeastern Spain** show the highest earthquake susceptibility, particularly in Granada, Almería, and Murcia provinces
2. **Mediterranean coastal regions** exhibit elevated risk due to proximity to the Eurasian-African plate boundary
3. **Central Iberian Peninsula** demonstrates relatively lower susceptibility
4. **Northern Spain** shows moderate to low risk levels except for specific localized zones
